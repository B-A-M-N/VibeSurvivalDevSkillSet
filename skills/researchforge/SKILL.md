# ResearchForge Skill

## Overview
ResearchForge is a comprehensive research and analysis skill for system design, verification, and validation. It orchestrates systematic investigation of requirements, specifications, and implementation details.

## Trigger
- `researchforge-overseer` during phase 1 (problem intake)
- `researchforge-analyzer` during phase 2 (context mapping)
- `researchforge-validator` during verification phases

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### 1. Problem Intake
- Parse initial requirements and constraints
- Identify key stakeholders and success criteria
- Establish verification goals

### 2. Context Mapping
- Analyze existing systems and dependencies
- Map data flows and integration points
- Identify risk factors and constraints

### 3. Evidence Collection
- Gather relevant documentation and specifications
- Collect existing test artifacts
- Interview stakeholders for requirements clarification

### 4. Source Quality Assessment
- Review code quality and architecture
- Identify technical debt and risks
- Assess test coverage adequacy

### 5. Hypothesis Generation
- Formulate testable hypotheses about system behavior
- Identify potential failure modes
- Design experiments to validate assumptions

### 6. Targeted Research Plan
- Develop detailed investigation strategy
- Prioritize research activities by risk and impact
- Allocate resources and timeline

### 7. Official Documentation Research
- Analyze existing official documentation
- Cross-reference with implementation
- Identify discrepancies and gaps

### 8. Upstream Issue Research
- Investigate related upstream projects and dependencies
- Track known issues and resolutions
- Assess compatibility implications

### 9. Version Compatibility Research
- Analyze version constraints and compatibility matrices
- Test different version combinations
- Document compatibility requirements

### 10. Architecture Pattern Research
- Study architectural patterns and best practices
- Evaluate alternative designs
- Recommend optimal architecture

### 11. Risk Research
- Identify and categorize risks
- Assess probability and impact
- Develop mitigation strategies

### 12. Contradiction Hunt
- Systematically search for requirement contradictions
- Validate assumptions against specifications
- Resolve ambiguities

### 13. Solution Option Synthesis
- Generate multiple solution approaches
- Analyze trade-offs and constraints
- Recommend optimal solutions

### 14. Validation Plan Generation
- Create comprehensive validation strategy
- Define test criteria and success metrics
- Plan verification activities

### 15. Final Research Packet
- Compile all research findings
- Create executive summary
- Document decisions and rationales

### 16. Adversarial Research Review
- Conduct peer review of research approach
- Challenge assumptions and conclusions
- Strengthen findings through critique

## Best Practices
- Always cross-reference with official specifications
- Maintain detailed documentation of research activities
- Use systematic approaches to problem-solving
- Validate findings through multiple independent methods
- Communicate results clearly to stakeholders
